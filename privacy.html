Privacy Policy

Football Timeline · Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Who we are

Football Timeline is a private tool used by the owner of the Football Timeline accounts to publish football highlight videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

2. What data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the service receives from each platform only what is needed to publish videos to that account:

  • access and refresh tokens issued by the platform;
  • basic account information (account ID, username, display name);
  • for TikTok: the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish, and the account's allowed privacy settings for posting.

We do not access, collect or process data of any other users, viewers or followers.

3. How we use it

The data is used only to upload and publish videos to the authorizing account and to check the result of the upload. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the server that runs the service, in files accessible solely to the account owner. They are never shared with or sold to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the service is in use and deleted when it is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services); after that the stored tokens stop working. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page.
